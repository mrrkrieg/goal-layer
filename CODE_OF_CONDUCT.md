# Code of conduct

Goal Layer contributors should be able to discuss product choices, report failures, and ask for help without harassment. Treat people respectfully across experience, identity, ability, background, and preferred working style.

Use constructive criticism focused on work and behavior. Respect privacy and consent. Do not post someone else's private goals, screenshots, activity, contact details, or credentials. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Participation in the project does not authorize promotional outreach or messages to other people.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or remove a contributor when necessary to protect the community. They should explain the relevant behavior and action to affected participants when doing so does not expose private reports or create further harm. Good-faith disagreements and reports should not lead to retaliation.

A private conduct-reporting destination has not yet been configured for this newly published source prototype. No invite community or active contributor cohort is claimed. The publication maintainer must establish and test that route before opening public participation. Until it is available, do not publish sensitive reports or identifying details in issue threads. The reporting destination and responsible maintainers will be recorded here when verified.

This policy applies to the project's issue tracker, code review, discussions, and other spaces maintained for Goal Layer. Future in-app communities need their own visible membership, moderation, reporting, blocking, and deletion controls under M6; this document does not claim those controls exist.
